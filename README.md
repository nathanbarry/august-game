# VELOCITY - Parkour Game

A fast-paced, neon-styled parkour platformer with 10 progressively challenging levels.

## How to Play

Simply open `index.html` in your web browser to start playing!

### Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| ← → or A/D | Move left/right |
| SPACE | Jump (press again in air for double jump) |
| ↓ or S | Slide (while moving) |
| SHIFT | Dash (quick burst of speed) |

### Advanced Moves

- **Wall Jump**: Jump while sliding down a wall (pink walls)
- **Double Jump**: Press jump again while in the air
- **Dash**: Quick horizontal burst - great for crossing gaps
- **Slide**: Lower your hitbox while maintaining momentum

## Levels

1. **First Steps** - Basic jumping tutorial
2. **Leap of Faith** - Longer jumps over gaps
3. **Danger Zone** - Introduction to spike hazards
4. **Wall Runner** - Learn wall jumping
5. **Keep Moving** - Moving platforms
6. **Needle Thread** - Precision jumps with spikes
7. **Vertical Limit** - Complex wall jumping
8. **Dash or Die** - Dashing is required
9. **Gauntlet** - Everything combined
10. **Velocity Master** - The ultimate challenge

## Features

- Smooth physics-based movement
- Wall jumping & sliding mechanics
- Dash ability with cooldown
- Moving platforms
- Particle effects
- Death counter & timer
- Cyberpunk neon aesthetic
